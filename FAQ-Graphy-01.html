Frequently Asked Questions
1. How do I Sign Up?
Click the Login button, then provide your phone number and email address. A welcome email will be sent to your registered email inbox.
2. How to purchase the course?
Click the Buy Course button, choose your preferred payment option, and complete the payment process.
3. Can I pay in any other currency?
Payment is accepted in India in Indian Rupees (INR). Outside India, it is the US Dollar (USD).
4. How do I access my course?
Once the payment is successful, the order confirmation and access details will be sent to your registered email address.
5. Is there any validity for the course?
Access is available from a single device for up to 365 days.
6. How to take the course support?
If you have any queries during the course, you can contact the trainer via chat or email.
7. Is the training practical oriented?
Yes, practical demonstrations and assignments are provided for most lessons wherever applicable.
8. Is study material included in the course?
Included in selected courses. Please contact us for more details.
9. Can I get any discount?
Yes, you may request a promo code, which may be available during festive seasons or special occasions.
10. Can I get a refund or transfer my course?
Once a course is purchased, it is non-refundable, non-transferable, and non-exchangeable under any circumstances.
11. Which version of the training is offered?
Different courses use different versions. You can verify it in the "About the Course" section. Please contact the trainer or take a demo session for more details.
12. Any questions unanswered?
Please check with the trainer for clarification before making a purchase, as details may change from time to time.
13. How to contact?
For any further information, please send us a message or an email. We’ll be happy to assist you.
